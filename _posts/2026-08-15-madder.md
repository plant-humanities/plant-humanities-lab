# Madder: The Ubiquitous Root of Red’s Entangled Histories

Fifteen thousand years ago, in the mountain ranges of the Levant, red-stained beads were being produced by a sedentary hunter-gatherer community.  The red color came from the roots of madder (Rubiaceae), a plant whose association with the color is now not known by many.
